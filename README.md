# LoopMind

### Repeat what you want your mind to remember.

LoopMind is a simple audio loop player for:

- 🎧 Affirmations
- 🧘 Meditation
- 🧠 Focus sessions
- 😴 Relaxation
- 🔁 Repeated audio practice

### Features

- Choose your own audio file
- 5, 10, 13, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60 and 90 minute sessions
- Custom session length
- Automatic audio looping
- Play, pause and stop controls
- Mobile-friendly
- Works on iPhone, Android and desktop
- Your audio stays on your device

> LoopMind does not upload or store your personal audio files.
